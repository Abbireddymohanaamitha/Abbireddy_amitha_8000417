Community News & Bulletin

Stay updated with the latest community news

Local Park Renovation Project Begins

The much-anticipated renovation of Riverside Park has officially commenced this month. Construction is expected to take 6-8 weeks and will include new walking trails, upgraded playground equipment, improved landscaping, and enhanced accessibility features. During this period, some areas of the park will be temporarily closed. We appreciate your patience and support as we work to create a more beautiful community space for everyone to enjoy.

New Community Center Opens Downtown

We are thrilled to announce the grand opening of our new Community Center located in the downtown district. This state-of-the-art facility features a fully equipped gym, swimming pool, dance studios, meeting rooms, and a cafeteria. The center is designed to serve people of all ages with various fitness and educational programs. The first month will feature special discounted memberships. Join us for the opening ceremony celebration with free activities, demonstrations, and refreshments.

Environmental Cleanup Initiative Recruitment

Our community is launching a comprehensive environmental cleanup initiative to keep our neighborhoods clean and green. We are recruiting volunteers for monthly cleanup drives on the first Saturday of each month. Whether you want to participate in street cleanups, park maintenance, or water conservation projects, there's a role for everyone. Training is provided, and all materials are supplied. Together, we can create a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable community for our children and future generations.

Youth Scholarship Program Now Open

The Community Foundation is pleased to announce that applications are now open for the annual Youth Scholarship Program. We are offering scholarships up to $5,000 for deserving high school and college students pursuing higher education. Applicants should demonstrate academic excellence, community involvement, and financial need. The application deadline is July 31, 2026. Winners will be announced in August and will have the opportunity to attend a recognition ceremony celebrating their achievements.

Small Business Support Workshop Series

Aspiring entrepreneurs and small business owners are invited to our comprehensive workshop series designed to help you start and grow your business. Topics include business planning, financial management, marketing strategies, digital presence, and networking opportunities. Workshops are held every Wednesday evening at the Community Center. Early registration is recommended as spaces fill up quickly. All workshops are free for community members, and light refreshments will be provided.

Senior Wellness Program Expansion

The Senior Wellness Program is expanding with new activities and services designed specifically for our senior community members. Activities include gentle yoga classes, walking groups, computer literacy classes, health seminars, and social gatherings. We believe in promoting healthy aging and maintaining an active lifestyle. All programs are accessible, affordable, and designed with seniors in mind. For more information and registration, visit the Community Center or call our senior services hotline.
